METHODOLOGY

Scientific Formulation.

How NutriGen determines your caloric requirements and portions meals.

01

BMR Estimation

Calculated via the Mifflin-St Jeor equation using sex, age, weight, and height to model resting basal metabolism.

02

TDEE Activity Multiplier

Physical activity coefficients (1.20 to 1.725) scale basal needs to reflect non-exercise activity and workout expenditure.

03

Energy Balance

Deficit (loss), surplus (gain), or equilibrium (maintenance) is calculated using a standard thermodynamic tolerance buffer.

04

Macro Portions

Nutrient ratios are balanced across 4 daily meals to stabilize blood glucose and promote sustained satiety.

Clinical & Educational Disclaimer

NutriGen is an algorithmic meal planning and lifestyle educational platform. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment plans, or clinical dietary prescriptions. Individuals with metabolic disorders, diabetes, renal conditions, or severe food allergies should consult a registered dietitian (RD) or primary physician before adopting any caloric deficit regimen.
