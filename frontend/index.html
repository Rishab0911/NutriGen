THE METHOD

How your plan was created.

NutriGen combines your information, goal and food preferences to estimate a practical daily nutrition target.

01

Your energy needs

Your age, height, weight and gender are used to estimate your basal energy requirements.

02

Your activity

Your selected activity level helps estimate how much energy you use throughout a typical day.

03

Your goal

Your selected goal adjusts the estimated target for weight loss, gain or maintenance.

04

Your food profile

Diet, preferences, allergies and restrictions influence which meals can appear in your plan.

Important note

These calculations are estimates for educational and planning purposes. They are not medical advice. Always verify ingredient labels and recipes yourself, especially if you have a serious allergy or medical condition.
